Natural gems formed over millions of years deep within the earth's crust and ocean depths carry an unyielding physical and energetic structure. When chosen according to your precise Kundali, a genuine gemstone never degrades, fades, or loses its intrinsic power, allowing it to be worn safely for an entire lifetime. Value Appreciation Facts for Planetary Gemstones

Key market insights explaining why Vedic-grade gemstones appreciate year over year

Blue Sapphire (Neelam / Saturn)

Fine Ceylon and Burma unheated blue sapphires have experienced steep price jumps due to depleting natural mines in Sri Lanka and rising worldwide demand for untreated Vedic-grade gems with verified origin certificates.

Kashmir Blue Sapphire (Neelam / Saturn)

Kashmir sapphires are considered the benchmark for extreme value appreciation. Because the original Himalayan mines closed in the late 1800s, supply is permanently fixed while global auction demand continues to skyrocket.

Emerald (Panna / Mercury)

Top-tier, untreated Colombian and fine Zambian emeralds with deep vivid green saturation and minimal oiling have historically outpaced standard inflation hedges, maintaining high liquidity over decades.

Natural Diamond (Heera / Venus)

Rare, unheated natural diamonds from legendary ancient origins carry an unparalleled historical markup. High-clarity natural Venus stones consistently appreciate at international auctions over two decades.

Yellow & Pink-Orange Sapphires

Natural Ceylon yellow sapphires and ultra-rare Padparadscha sapphires experience steady price compounding because untreated sunset-toned specimens account for less than 1% of the total global gem trade.

Yellow Sapphire (Pukhraj / Jupiter)

Sri Lankan mining regulations restricting automated bulk extractions make untreated natural yellow sapphires scarce, steadily driving per-carat retail costs upward year after year.

Natural Ruby (Manikya / Sun)

Fine unheated Burmese and old-mine rubies (Pigeon Blood Red) command extraordinary valuation growth due to depleting Asian deposits, protecting capital against currency devaluation.

Natural Pearl (Moti / Moon)

Genuine, certified Basra pearls from the Persian Gulf oyster beds are permanently fixed in supply after historic bed exhaustion, making authentic natural pearls prized collector items.

Natural Red Coral (Moonga / Mars)

Italian Mediterranean and Japanese deep-sea untreated red corals have seen consistent price increases due to strict environmental harvesting caps restricting natural raw supplies.

When You MUST NOT Wear a Gemstone

Strict Caution Required
  • For Malefic / Trika Houses: NEVER wear gems for planets owning 6th, 8th, or 12th houses without expert consultation.

  • Without Horoscope Verification: Never wear a gemstone solely based on Sun Sign, Moon Sign, or general internet recommendations.
